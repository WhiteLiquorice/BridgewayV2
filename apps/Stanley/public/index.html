Common Questions

Real answers to the questions you'd actually ask before trusting a daemon on your machine.

Does Stanley violate the terms of service of websites I use it on?
Stanley is a general-purpose browser automation tool — similar to using a macro or keyboard-shortcut program. How you use it is your responsibility. We recommend reviewing a site's terms before automating interactions with it. Stanley does not rotate IPs, spoof headers, or misrepresent your identity. It controls a real browser on your own machine the same way you would manually.
What operating system does Stanley support?
Windows 10 and Windows 11 only. The desktop daemon is a Windows-native executable (.exe). macOS and Linux support are on the roadmap but not yet available. Please do not purchase if you are on a Mac or Linux machine.
What happens immediately after I check out?
After your Stripe payment completes you are redirected to a secure setup page. From there you download the Windows daemon (.exe) and Chrome extension (.zip), then create your Stanley account credentials in about 60 seconds. Your purchase ID is shown on the Stripe receipt in case you ever need to re-access the setup page.
Is there a refund policy?
Yes. If Stanley does not work as described on your Windows machine within the first 7 days of your subscription, contact us at contact@bridgewayapps.com for a full refund — no questions asked.
What does Stanley actually install on my machine?
The daemon accesses only what it needs to run your workflows: it launches a Chromium process, reads task instructions from the Chrome extension via Chrome's native messaging protocol, and writes output (e.g. scraped data) to a local JSON file. It does not access your files, passwords, clipboard, camera, microphone, or any other system resource. All data stays on your machine. You can review the full privacy policy here.
